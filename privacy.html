Privacy Policy

Effective . Muffin Night is operated by ("we", "us").

The short version. What you and your partner write, answer, rate, and share is encrypted on your phones with a passphrase only the two of you know. We can't read it. We don't sell data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. A few things are needed to run the app (your sign-in email, notification settings) and a few you can choose to share (anonymous counts, feedback, messages to the AI coach). This page explains each one.

1. What we collect, and why

  • Your account. When you sign in with Google or an email link, we receive your email address and an account ID from Google Firebase. We use them to sign you in, link you to your partner, and contact you about your account.
  • Your couple space. Survey answers, notes, ratings, memories, photos, plans, check-ins, and everything else you two create is encrypted on your device before it's stored, using your couple passphrase. We store only the encrypted version and cannot read it. We also store which accounts belong to which couple.
  • Flirty photos are stored encrypted in your couple space and are deleted automatically after 7 days.
  • Feedback. If you send feedback with the 💬 button, we receive the text you write, your email, and your account and couple IDs, so we can follow up. Only send what you're comfortable with us reading.
  • Anonymous counts (optional, off unless you say yes). If you opt in, the app sends counts such as which screens and features are used, how cards are rated, check-in levels, survey topic totals, and monthly pulse scores. They're filed under a random couple number that isn't linked to your name, email, or account. If you choose, it also includes your age range, years together, relationship status, kids at home, region, and how often you're intimate. You can turn this off at any time in More, Guide.
  • AI coach (optional). When you use the coach, your messages, and your profile summary if you include it, are sent from your phone through our server to Anthropic, which provides the AI model, to generate a reply. We don't store your messages; we keep only a daily count to limit use. Your chat history stays on your phone. Anthropic handles those messages under its commercial terms; see Anthropic's privacy center for how it uses API data.
  • Notifications (optional). If you turn them on, we store your device's push token, your reminder preferences, your time zone, and the times of scheduled reminders (such as a surprise moment). They never contain anything you or your partner wrote.
  • On your device. The app keeps settings, drafts, and your coach chat in your phone's local storage. Clearing the app's data on your phone removes them.
  • Technical logs. Our hosting and cloud providers may log basic technical information such as IP address, browser type, and time of request, to keep the service secure and working.

2. Sensitive information

Muffin Night is about your relationship and sex life, which many laws treat as sensitive. By using the app, you consent to it handling this information as described here. Because your couple space is encrypted with your passphrase, that information is not readable by us. The only sensitive information we can see is what you choose to send us as feedback, what you send to the AI coach (which passes through to Anthropic), and the anonymous counts and optional details described above if you opt in.

3. Who we share with

We don't sell or rent your information, and we don't share it for advertising. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Google (Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore, Cloud Run, and Firebase Cloud Messaging) for sign-in, storage, our server, and notifications.
  • GitHub (GitHub Pages) to host the app's web pages.
  • Anthropic for AI coach replies, only when you use the coach.
  • Google Fonts to load the app's typefaces.

We may also disclose information if the law requires it, to protect someone's safety, or as part of a sale or reorganization of the business. Since we can't read your encrypted couple space, we can't hand over its contents.

4. Your partner

Things you choose to share in the app, such as notes, sparks, answers you reveal, and shared survey results, are visible to your partner. Your private answers (like your "not for me" answers, unless you share them) are kept from your partner by the app's design. Anyone who knows your couple passphrase and can sign in to one of your accounts can see what that account can see.

5. How long we keep things

We keep your account and encrypted couple space until you ask us to delete them. Feedback is kept as long as it's useful for improving the app. Anonymous counts can't be linked back to you, so they can't be removed individually and are kept to understand how the app is used.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Turn anonymous counts, notifications, and the coach on or off at any time.
  • Ask us to access, correct, or delete your account information, or to delete your account and your couple space, by emailing . If you live in a state or country with additional privacy rights, such as California, Washington, the EU, or the UK, you can use them the same way. We won't treat you differently for using them.
  • Lost passphrase: we can't recover it, and without it the encrypted couple space can't be read. Keep a backup.

7. Security

Your couple space is encrypted on your device with AES-GCM, using a key derived from your passphrase. Access to our database is limited by security rules, and only the operator account can read feedback and anonymous counts. No system is perfectly secure, so pick a strong passphrase and keep your phone locked.

8. Adults only

Muffin Night is only for adults 18 and older. We don't knowingly collect information from anyone under 18. If we learn that someone under 18 has an account, we'll delete it.

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top and, for important changes, ask you to review it in the app.
